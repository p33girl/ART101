Black is the best color
but pink is the prettiest;
My last name is Green
My middle is from my mother

I love Nirvana and Hole,
Soundgarden, Smashing Pumpkins,
all kinds of ear-splitting rock 'n' roll...

In Utero, Nevermind,
Live Through This and Pretty on the Inside,
Voices of people who have lived and died

Halloween is my favorite night
of costumes, freaks, and fright;
When the occult runs free,
it means horror movies and candy

I've been around for 20 spins
Much longer in dog years;
Two more at this school, drinking beers
A film degree, I'll win

Love is art and art is love,
Exercises of the soul
That I'll cherish as I grow old,
Until the day I find myself
Sleeping down in a hole.
